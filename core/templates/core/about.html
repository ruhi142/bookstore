Our Story

Books deserve a shelf
that actually knows you.

Inkwell Books started with a simple frustration: browsing for a book online felt like scrolling a warehouse catalogue, not walking into a bookstore. So we built one — new titles, quality-checked pre-loved copies, and a little AI help when you're not sure what you're in the mood for.

What We Believe

Four ideas run through everything we build

Curated, not just catalogued

Every category is shaped by actual reading trends and reviews — not just what has the highest margin. If it's on the shelf, someone here would recommend it.

Fair, transparent pricing

The price you see on a listing is the price you pay — discounts are shown as real cuts off MRP, never inflated first and "discounted" after.

Security by default

Every checkout is OTP-verified and every order is trackable end to end. We treat "trustworthy" as a feature we owe you, not an add-on.

A second life for good books

Our pre-loved section is graded and photographed honestly, so a book gets read again instead of sitting shut on someone's shelf.
